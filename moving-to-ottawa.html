Moving to Ottawa

Whether you’re relocating for work, arriving in Canada, or moving on a military posting, we help you find the right neighbourhood and the right home, often before you’ve set foot in the city.

Relocating to Ottawa for work

Ottawa’s big employers are spread out: federal departments downtown and in Gatineau, the Kanata North tech park in the west end, hospitals and universities in the core. Where you work shapes where you’ll want to live.

  • A shortlist of neighbourhoods that fit your commute, schools and budget, before you visit
  • Video tours and live walk-throughs when you can’t be here in person
  • A house-hunting trip planned to see the most homes in the fewest days
  • If you’re selling elsewhere, timing your purchase and closing dates so the move lines up
  • Trusted local mortgage brokers, lawyers, inspectors and movers
  • Renting first? Help finding a lease while you get to know the city

Newcomers to Canada

Buying or renting your first home in a new country comes with new rules and new words. We explain each step in plain language and take the time to answer every question.

  • How buying works in Ontario, from offer to closing day, and who does what
  • Getting a mortgage without a long Canadian credit history, through lenders with newcomer programs
  • Down payments, closing costs and land transfer tax, worked out before you shop
  • Federal rules on who can buy residential property in Canada, and the exemptions, checked for your situation before you start
  • First-time buyer programs and tax savings you may qualify for
  • Renting first while you settle in, with help on applications and references

Military postings

Posted to National Defence Headquarters at the Carling campus, CFS Leitrim or another Ottawa unit? Or posted out and need to sell? Military moves run on fixed dates, so we plan around yours.

  • House-hunting trips planned to make the most of the days you’re given
  • Offers and closing dates set around your report date
  • Help with the paperwork your relocation provider asks for
  • Neighbourhoods close to your unit: Kanata, Bells Corners and Westboro for Carling; Findlay Creek and Riverside South for Leitrim
  • Selling when you’re posted out, including selling while you’re already away
  • Renting out your home instead, if you expect to come back to Ottawa

Common questions

Can you help me buy a home in Ottawa before I move there?

Yes. We shortlist neighbourhoods and homes for you, tour them by live video, and handle offers and paperwork remotely, so many clients buy before they arrive.

Which Ottawa neighbourhoods are good for families relocating?

It depends on where you’ll work and your budget. Kanata, Stittsville, Barrhaven, Findlay Creek, Riverside South and Orléans are popular family suburbs, while the Glebe and Westboro suit people who want to walk to shops and downtown.

Can newcomers to Canada get a mortgage?

Many lenders have programs for newcomers, including permanent residents and some work permit holders, even without a long Canadian credit history. We connect you with mortgage specialists who work with newcomers.

I’ve been posted to Ottawa. How quickly can you help me find a home?

We can start as soon as you have your posting message. We plan your house-hunting trip around the days you’re given and set closing dates to match your report date.

Do you help people rent in Ottawa as well as buy?

Yes. Many people rent first while they get to know the city, and we help with finding and applying for leases.

Planning a move to Ottawa?

Tell us your timing and what matters most, and we’ll send a neighbourhood shortlist to get you started.
